# What does the effort screen look like with a conductor?

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 02
Map: [Each effort has a conductor](../map.md)

## Question

With a conductor running the effort, what do the middle and right panels show? Make a rough, clickable prototype in the KiPo harness to react to. It should cover:

- The conductor's chat: what it says on its own (what it started, merged or noticed) versus what it says when you talk to it.
- "Needs you": where your items appear, how they're answered, and how they differ from the cards the ticket conversations raise.
- What happens to the Autopilot toggle and the "Next" section on the right.
- How you see what the conductor is doing right now (running workers, the last time it woke) without reading its whole transcript.

Keep the one-place rule from the redesign: the middle is for moving around, talking and acting; the right is read-only facts plus the single next action.
